# Rooftop AI Studio website: design

Date: 2026-11-23. Status: waiting for Pini's approval.

## Intent

The site runs under the studio name **Rooftop AI Studio** (chosen by Pini; first address `rooftop-ai-studio.vercel.app`, own domain later). Pini Zolberg is named as the person behind it.

Pini Zolberg builds AI-powered digital products for businesses, privately and alone: websites, brands, apps, games, custom platforms, brand videos, agents, second brains, AI workflows, RAG and fine-tuning.

The site has one job. A business owner who lands on it should think "that's the guy for me" within seconds, and then open a WhatsApp chat with Pini.

The site is also the proof. It must be spectacular: real 3D, motion and interaction at a level that shows Pini's design and engineering ability by itself.

A second, minor job: it is the public developer website for Pini's Google Play Console account, so it hosts the privacy and contact pages Google requires.

## Positioning (decided with Pini)

One promise instead of a service list: **"Your business, rebuilt for the AI era: a new face and a new brain."**

- **The face** is everything customers see: brand, website, app, game, video, custom platform.
- **The brain** is everything that runs behind: agents, second brain, AI workflows, and deeper work such as RAG and fine-tuning.

The site speaks to a business owner, in results, about his own business. Technology terms appear only in the section written for technical buyers.

## Goals

- The first screen asks the visitor what he runs, and from then on the 3D scene and every line of copy are about that business.
- Several distinct spectacular moments, not one hero effect (see "The page").
- A real AI on the site that gives the visitor something of value he can keep: a personal plan for his business.
- Real work shown honestly, each piece labelled as client work, own product or prototype.
- One clear action everywhere: talk to Pini on WhatsApp, with the visitor's business and plan already in the message.

## Non-goals (for this version)

- Tuning for weak devices and phones. Pini's instruction: build the best thing possible first. The site must still open and be readable on a phone, but the 3D experience is designed for a good desktop or laptop.
- Hebrew. English first; all copy lives in one place so Hebrew (right-to-left) can be added later.
- A blog, pricing page or client login.

## Anti-goals

- Invented numbers, testimonials or client logos. Every claim on the site is true.
- Any mention of Pini's employer.
- Pini's real personal data in product demos. Demos use staged data.
- A template look: identical rounded cards, fade-up on every section, generic stock 3D blobs.
- Motion that traps the visitor. He can always scroll on, skip, and reach the WhatsApp button.
- A loading screen with nothing to look at.

## Approaches considered

Three concepts were sketched and shown to Pini as clickable mockups:

| Concept | Idea | Trade-off |
|---|---|---|
| A. Becomes what you need | Visitor picks his need and the page transforms into it | Most personal; needs strong content per choice |
| B. One idea, all the way | One made-up business followed from scribble to running company | Best story; same story for every visitor; most build time |
| C. Play with it | A shelf of small working things to poke | Most fun; weak on "what do I get" |

**Picked:** A as the spine, in real 3D, re-aimed from "pick a service" to "pick your business". C becomes the work section. B becomes the short "how it works" film. A throwaway 3D prototype of the opening confirmed the direction; Pini asked for a far more spectacular build, a 3D model that changes per business, and more than one spectacular moment.

On wording, "make your business ready for AI" was considered and replaced by the face-and-brain promise, because "ready" sounds like preparation, not results, and does not cover games, brands and videos.

## The page

One long home page made of scenes, in the order a business owner asks his questions.

### 1. Opening: the living model

- Headline: the promise. Under it: "Show me yours. I run a…" with choices: Restaurant, Clinic, Shop, Studio or agency, Something else.
- The 3D subject is a **detailed miniature of that business**, floating in space: a restaurant, a clinic, a shop, a studio. "Something else" shows an abstract building.
- The model is made of solid stylised geometry plus a dense field of light particles. Changing business makes the model tear apart into particles, swirl, and re-form as the new business. This is the signature moment.
- The mouse orbits the model and pushes the particles.
- The accent colour, lighting and all copy on the page change with the business.

### 2. The dive: face to brain

- Scrolling opens the model's outside. Each outer piece is one "face" item and turns to show a real design for that business: its website, its app, its brand, its video.
- The camera then travels through the opening into the inside: a living core with agents moving along glowing paths. Each path is one named job in plain words ("answers and books, day and night"). Pointing at a path shows what that agent is doing.
- Copy alongside lists three face results and three brain results for the chosen business.

### 3. Your plan, live (the real AI)

- The visitor types one sentence about his business and its name.
- A real AI writes, as he watches, a short personal plan: three face moves, three brain moves, and the one thing to do first.
- While it writes, the 3D model reacts: the business name appears on the model's sign and the palette shifts.
- The result is a clean card he can save or share, with a button: "Send this plan to Pini on WhatsApp".
- Protection: limits per visitor, a daily spending cap, and nothing personal stored. When the cap is reached or the AI is unavailable, the site shows a prepared plan for the chosen business type and says so.

### 4. The work

- Real projects as objects in a 3D gallery the visitor can spin. Opening one fills the screen with a case: what the business needed, what was built, shown on 3D devices with real screens, and an honest label.
- Projects (Pini's list first):
  - **Maslul**: delivery platform for a bakery business; drivers find exactly where to leave goods at night. Client-type work.
  - **Hair of Greece Recovery Companion**: a clinic's patient companion for recovery after a procedure. Client-type work.
  - **Toran**: WhatsApp booking AI for barbers and nail studios. Prototype, labelled as one.
  - **Gudauri ski app**: four-language site and native Android app with a 3D map and games. Own product.
  - **Home OS**: a family AI agent on WhatsApp with one shared memory. Own product; shown without private details.
  - Chosen from the other repos: **Sari Steiner** architecture portfolio (brand and website), the **challah bakery** film (video and brand), **Pensionview** (AI that reads documents, staged data), **Ba Li Mashehu** (a decision game).

### 5. How it works

- A short pinned film in four steps, because it is a real sequence: Talk, Plan, Build, Run. One object transforms through the steps as the visitor scrolls.

### 6. Under the hood (for the technical buyer)

- An interactive map of what Pini builds on the brain side: agents, second brains and RAG, workflows, fine-tuning, evaluation. Each opens to one plain line and one technical line.

### 7. About Pini

- Short and personal: one person who designs and engineers the whole thing. No employer. Trust comes from the work above.

### 8. Finale

- The particles write the visitor's business name: "Let's build ___." One button: WhatsApp.

### Across the whole page

- One orchestrated loading-to-opening sequence.
- Smooth scrolling, scene-to-scene camera moves, a custom cursor.
- Optional sound, off by default.
- A fixed "Talk to me" WhatsApp button, always reachable.

### Quiet pages for Google Play

- Privacy policy, a per-app privacy page template, account deletion request, contact. Plain, fast, no 3D, linked from the footer.

## Look

- Base: deep indigo night `#0B0920`, ivory text `#F4EFFF`.
- Accent follows the business: ember `#FF7A45` (restaurant), mint `#3DD6C4` (clinic), pink `#FF4F9A` (shop), violet `#8C7BFF` (studio), gold `#FFC14D` (default).
- Type: Syne (wide, heavy) for headlines, Instrument Sans for reading.
- Boldness is spent on the 3D and motion. Layout and text around it stay quiet and left-aligned.

## How it gets built (Claude's calls, not yet reviewed by Pini)

- Next.js with TypeScript, because Pini's other projects use it and the text must be findable by search engines.
- React Three Fiber with custom shaders and post-processing for the 3D; GSAP ScrollTrigger and Lenis for scroll-driven motion.
- Hosted on Vercel, where Pini's other sites live. The AI plan runs as a small streaming server function calling a fast, low-cost Claude model.
- 3D models: start from free-licence stylised model kits restyled with custom materials, and test AI-generated models against them. Quality is judged at milestone 1.
- Contact: a WhatsApp link with a prefilled message.
- Tests: few and meaningful, on the AI plan endpoint and its limits.

## Milestones

0. **Google Play pages first** (built): a simple studio home page, privacy policy, contact and account-deletion request, live on Vercel. Contact is by email until the WhatsApp number is given.
1. **The opening scene alone**, at full quality, with per-business models and the particle morph. Pini approves the look before anything else is built.
2. The dive and the live AI plan.
3. The work gallery with the real projects, then scenes 5 to 8.
4. Full launch.

## Calls made after the builder review (Claude's, for Pini to check)

- **Model style.** Stylised, glowing architectural miniatures with real detail, not low-poly toys and not photo-real. The rejected prototype is at `.superpowers/brainstorm/75588-1795434497/content/hero-prototype-v3.html`: a ring of flat placeholder cards around a glowing blob. It fell short because the model was abstract, identical for every business, and low in detail. Milestone 1 settles the style.
- **Designs inside the dive.** One invented brand per business type (restaurant, clinic, shop, studio). For each: a high-quality still of its website, its app and its brand, and one short looping video. "Something else" uses a neutral set. That is 16 designed pieces and they are part of milestone 2.
- **Phones.** Same content and order. The 3D scenes are replaced by pre-rendered video of the same scenes. The business picker, the live AI plan and the WhatsApp button must work on a phone.
- **Hebrew typing.** Site copy is English, but visitors may type in Hebrew. The plan answers in the language typed, and the 3D sign and finale text use a typeface that includes Hebrew and handle right-to-left.
- **Sharing the plan.** Download as an image and send by WhatsApp only. No share links, so nothing is stored on the server.
- **Google Play pages.** A general developer privacy policy now; a per-app page is added when each app is published. Account deletion is a short form that sends the request to Pini.
- **Project labels.** Each project's label (paid client, built for a real business, own product, prototype) and whether the business may be named are confirmed with Pini before that project goes on the site. Until confirmed, a project is shown unnamed.
